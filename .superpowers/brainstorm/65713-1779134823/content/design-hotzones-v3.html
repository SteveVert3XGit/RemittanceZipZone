Hot Zone Algorithm — v3: Demand-Adjusted Classification

Competitors validate demand. Saturation is WU density relative to that demand — not an absolute count.

Two-Axis Model

WU SUPPLY DENSITY →
HIGH DEMAND · HIGH WU
⚠ MONITOR
WU density justified by strong demand + competitor validation. Watch for new competitor entry.
LOW DEMAND · HIGH WU
▼ REDUCE
WU oversaturated relative to market size. No competitor validation of demand. Consolidate.
HIGH DEMAND · LOW/NO WU
▲ EXPAND
Strong demand signal + competitor presence confirms market. WU missing = clear gap.
LOW DEMAND · LOW/NO WU
— NEUTRAL
No opportunity signal. No action needed. No zone rendered on map.

Adjusted Demand Score Formula

DEMAND SIGNALS (within 0.3mi cluster radius)
🟣 Ethnic Grocery / Supermarket+3
🟢 Financial Services+2
🩷 Dollar Stores+1
🔵 Laundromats+1
COMPETITOR VALIDATION BONUS (within 0.5mi)
🔵 MoneyGram present+2
🔴 Ria present+2
Competitors add to demand score because their presence proves remittance customers already exist in that cluster — they chose to locate there for a reason.

Classification Thresholds

// adjDemand = sum of all signals above within radius
// wuCount = Western Union locations within 0.5mi

EXPAND   : adjDemand ≥ 5 AND wuCount == 0
MONITOR  : adjDemand ≥ 5 AND wuCount ≥ 2
REDUCE   : adjDemand < 4 AND wuCount ≥ 2
NEUTRAL  : everything else — no zone rendered
