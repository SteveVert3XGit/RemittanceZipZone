Custom SVG Map

Hand-crafted per ZIP. Works offline and has no CDN dependency, but every new ZIP requires manually redrawing streets and recalculating coordinates.

Why this matters

Since we're building data-driven and scaling to multiple ZIPs, Leaflet eliminates the biggest scaling bottleneck. The original SVG approach required hand-drawing every street — Leaflet renders them automatically from OpenStreetMap tiles.
